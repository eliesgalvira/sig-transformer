# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Students, engineers, and technically curious users exploring how sampled waveforms appear in the frequency domain. They work directly in the Signal Workbench, iterating on waveform parameters and comparing the resulting transform without leaving the page.

## Product Purpose

Signal Workbench turns an editable Signal Draft into a Committed Signal and a readable FFT visualization. Success means users can change parameters, generate a signal, and inspect the result with a stable, responsive workflow that keeps the relationship between inputs and output clear.

## Brand Personality

Precise, calm, technical. The interface should feel like dependable lab equipment: purposeful enough to support experimentation, restrained enough that the signal remains the focus.

## Anti-references

Avoid flashy dashboard decoration, full-chart loading interruptions, gratuitous glow and motion, and visual effects that compete with the data. Avoid generic marketing-page composition and playful controls that obscure standard form behavior.

## Design Principles

- Keep the signal and its transform as the primary visual evidence.
- Preserve context while work is in progress; do not blank useful results during regeneration.
- Make parameter constraints discoverable and consistent wherever a Signal Draft is edited.
- Prefer familiar, compact controls suited to repeated technical work.
- Use motion only to communicate a meaningful state change.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast and keyboard-operable controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences, do not rely on color alone for state, and keep technical labels and values legible across desktop and mobile layouts.
